Owners often arrive with a style in mind and a number in mind. The useful question is whether the existing house can carry the life you want, or whether the lot is the asset and the building is in the way.

## What a renovation can still do

If structure, layout and systems are close to the plan, a whole-home renovation can reset kitchens, baths, envelope and interiors without a full rebuild. You keep legal non-conforming advantages in some cases. You also inherit whatever is in the walls: wiring, plumbing, insulation, moisture. Discovery during demolition is normal. Budget for it.

## When a custom home is cleaner

If you need a different footprint, a second dwelling, or a multiplex, renovation math often collapses. New construction puts zoning, energy code and warranty on a current baseline. It also means living elsewhere for a stretch. That cost belongs in the comparison, not as a surprise.

## How to decide without a brochure

Walk the property with someone who builds both paths. Bring how many households will live there, whether rental suites matter, and a range you can live with. The [project estimator](/estimator) is a planning figure only: floor area times a rate. Drawings, soils, finishes and municipal conditions set the real number.
